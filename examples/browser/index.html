Gestures and the keymap

    
Tap, triple-tap or hold a button (or use the keys 1, 2, 3). The gesture engine names what you did; the keymap says what it means for the kind of prompt showing. Buttons 1 and 3 have a triple tap, so a single tap on them is reported half a second late.

The LED matrix

    
13 by 8 lamps and three RGB LEDs. The engine makes the same frame, byte for byte, as the device does for the same inputs.
